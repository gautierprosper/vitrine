About Us

The planning software that enforces deadlines. Simple, visual and professional for construction teams.

What is PlannInTime?

PlannInTime lets you create in minutes clear and professional construction schedules: tasks, durations, dependencies, Gantt chart and site tracking.

No more complicated Excel files. With PlannInTime, you save time and present a clean planning to your clients, your team and your partners.

Who are we?

PlannInTime is a group of experts passionate about management and information technology, who develop every day simple, practical and professional solutions to help companies, craftsmen, site managers and entrepreneurs to better organize their work.

We started from a very simple observation: many companies, especially in construction and building, lose time and money because of poor organization. Poorly made schedules, forgotten tasks, missed deadlines, complicated Excel files that nobody understands. We decided to change that.

That is how PlannInTime was born. Not just an application, but a real working method. A method designed for the field, for people who really work on construction sites, in offices, in workshops. Not for computer scientists, but for managers, site supervisors, team leaders, SME owners.

Every day, our team works on three pillars:

1. Understanding the real need.
Before creating an application, we go to the field. We listen to site managers, stock managers, accountants, HR managers. We look at how they really work, what their blockages are, their waste of time. Because a good application is not a complicated application, it is an application that solves a real problem.

2. Designing simple and powerful tools.
Our specialty is to transform a complicated need into a simple tool. A construction schedule made in 2 clicks. A dashboard that reads in 10 seconds. An Android application that works even without connection. We believe technology must serve people, not the other way around.

3. Training and supporting.
Creating an application is not enough. People need to know how to use it. That is why we created That is why we created Le Gestionnaire Professionnel. A channel and a community to show you step by step, from A to Z, how to design your own management applications: stocks, sales, accounting, HR, finance, procurement.

Behind PlannInTime, there is Prosper, founder of Le Gestionnaire Professionnel, and a whole team of experts in management, IT and teaching. Together, we have only one goal: to save you time, to make you more professional, and to help you present clean, clear and credible work to your clients.

Today, PlannInTime is used to plan construction sites, assign tasks, track deadlines, export professional schedules in A4 / A3 PDF, and receive automatic alerts at D-3, D-1. Tomorrow, we want to go even further: more languages, more features, more tools for you.

PlannInTime is not just software. It is your daily management partner.
