What is included for $0?

Everything you need to run a real construction site. Create your project in minutes, add your tasks, assign your workers, set your deadlines, and let PlannInTime calculate your timeline automatically. You get automatic notifications at D-3, D-1 and H-2 before each task, so your teams never miss a deadline. Why is it free?

We built PlannInTime because most planning software is too expensive and too complicated for small and medium construction teams. In many countries, site managers still plan with Excel, paper, or WhatsApp, and lose hours every week. Our mission is to give every builder in the world a simple, professional, and free planning tool. During the launch period, all features are free. In the future, some advanced premium features for large companies may become optional, but the core planning tool will always stay free.

Who is the free plan for?

The free plan is for site managers, contractors, engineers, architects, and project owners who want a clear and professional schedule without spending hours on Excel. It works for houses, buildings, villas, schools, roads, renovations, and any construction project, wherever you build in the world. If you manage several projects at the same time, you can create them all for free, with no limit.
